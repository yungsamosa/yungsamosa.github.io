About

I study economics and data science at UChicago, with a minor in history. Across everything I have worked on the pattern is the same: work out which question actually matters, then build whatever pipeline, model, or spreadsheet answers it. That has taken me through a strategy team at eBay, consumer insights at SteelSeries, and private equity diligence in Detroit.

The rest of my time goes to consulting and competitions. I consult for nonprofits through campusCATALYST, where I built an expansion blueprint for growth into underserved Southern communities and brought in two clients for the next project cycle. Before that I did early operations work at Renest Horizons, interviewing forty-odd students to sharpen their customer personas and pitching the product to university leadership. In 2025 I placed first at the BCG x Prism case competition, second at MMSI x McKinsey, and reached the final at Bain.

Away from a screen: weightlifting, tabla, backpacking, cooking, and Phoenix Farms, a student farming club where I mostly mix neem oil pesticide and sell pickles. Eagle Scout, 2024.
